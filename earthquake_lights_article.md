## Key precursor relations (from §13)

| Class | Relation | Equation |
|---|---|---|
| First-kind precursors (crustal anomalies) | log₁₀T(days) = −1.08 + 0.59 M | (1) |
| Quasi-first-kind (geochemical / geo-EM) | log₁₀T(days) = −0.89 + 0.38 M | (2) |
| EQL precursors (34 testimonies, 4 major quakes) | log₁₀T(days) = −3.71 + 0.48 M | (3) |

EQL precursor times are shorter than both other classes — possibly enabling near-immediate prediction via IR surveillance.
